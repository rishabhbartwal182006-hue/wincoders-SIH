THE CORE PITCH LINE:
“We don’t need altitude hardware. We need altitude context. The kiosk already captures symptoms and vitals. Our MVP adds a configurable altitude profile and rule engine that interprets readings in context, while preserving raw data and keeping the clinician in control.”

========================================================================================
1. THE PROBLEM (30 SECONDS)
========================================================================================
- In high-altitude clinics—from Leh and Ladakh (3,500m) to Shimla, Ooty, and military border posts—standard medical devices are calibrated for sea level.
- Normal SpO2 at 8,000 ft drops from 98% down to 92-95% due to reduced barometric atmospheric pressure.
- Without elevation context:
  * Either healthy acclimatizing tourists trigger false panics and overwhelm doctors,
  * OR critically deteriorating patients suffering high-altitude pulmonary edema (HAPE) or acute mountain sickness (AMS) are missed because basic numbers look "ambiguous".
- Clinics don't have budget to buy specialized $10,000 altitude sensors for every kiosk.

========================================================================================
2. OUR SOLUTION (45 SECONDS)
========================================================================================
- MediKiosk introduces Altitude Contextual Clinical Decision Support (CDS):
  1. Configurable Altitude Profiles: Automatically loaded from facility elevation config (e.g. 2,438 m) or manual staff entry.
  2. Contextual Clinical Interpretation: Dynamic SpO2 expected ranges without ever distorting normal blood pressure thresholds (120/80 mmHg remains strictly normal).
  3. Strict Raw Value Preservation: Raw patient sensor readings are permanently stored in ABDM/FHIR R4 Observation resources.
  4. Explainable Multi-Symptom Rule Engine: Combines elevation + vitals + danger symptoms (chest tightness, dyspnea, confusion) to escalate critical red flags.
  5. Physician Command & Override: The doctor retains full clinical authority. Overrides require mandatory HPR (Healthcare Professional Registry) biometric write-lock and generate an append-only, tamper-evident SHA-256 cryptographically chained audit log.

[SCENARIO A: Sea Level Severe Hypoxemia]
Command:
  node demo/load_scenario.js --scenario=a
What Judges See:
  - Altitude: 0 meters (Sea Level)
  - Vitals: SpO2 = 88%, BP = 120/80 mmHg
  - Symptoms: None (routine health checkup)
  - Clinical Verdict: RED FLAG (Severe hypoxemia at sea level).
  - Rationale: At sea level, expected SpO2 is 95-100%. 88% is >5% below expected minimum. Immediate doctor alert.

[SCENARIO B: High Altitude (8,000 ft / 2,438 m) with Danger Symptom]
Command:
  node demo/load_scenario.js --scenario=b
What Judges See:
  - Altitude: 2,438 m (8,000 ft)
  - Vitals: SpO2 = 88%, BP = 120/80 mmHg
  - Symptoms: "Chest tightness" & fatigue
  - Clinical Verdict:
    * Blood Pressure: 120/80 mmHg is interpreted as NORMAL. We NEVER shift BP thresholds upward for elevation.
    * SpO2: 88% is mildly below the 92-97% altitude band, but because "chest tightness" is present, Rule 1 escalates to CRITICAL RED FLAG ("Hypoxemia at altitude with danger symptoms").
    * Doctor Fast-Track: Instant Tele-triage notification pushed to Doctor Command Center via WebSockets.
